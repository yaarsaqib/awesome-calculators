## Calculator-use checklist

Before trusting a numerical result:

1. Identify the physical system and governing principle.
2. Convert quantities to consistent units.
3. Preserve signs and vector directions.
4. Keep enough precision during intermediate steps.
5. Check the final units and order of magnitude.
6. Compare the result with the diagram, graph, or limiting behavior.

A calculator can execute arithmetic; it cannot decide whether the chosen equation, system boundary, sign convention, or physical model is appropriate.
